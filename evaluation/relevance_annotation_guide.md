# Relevance Annotation Guide

Judge each paper by how directly its content addresses the query's research topic. Do not use its retrieval rank or score as evidence of relevance.

- Enter `1` for relevant and `0` for not relevant. Leave the field blank until reviewed.
- Apply the same judgment to a paper wherever it appears for the same query, regardless of retrieval mode.
- Use `annotation_notes` for brief context or uncertainty.
- `query_id` is the 1-based query index from `annotation_candidates.json`.
